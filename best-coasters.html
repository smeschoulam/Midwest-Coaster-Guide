Best Coasters in the Midwest

Our fictional editorial team's five favorite rides for speed, airtime, intensity, and unforgettable experiences.

Five Midwest Roller Coasters Worth the Trip

The Midwest is home to some of the most exciting roller coasters in the country. Whether you love powerful launches, wooden coaster chaos, or massive drops, there is something here for every thrill seeker.

Our rankings reflect the imagined preferences of the Heartland Thrill Co. editorial team. They are subjective recommendations, not official industry awards.

#1 Overall Favorite

1. Steel Vengeance — Cedar Point, Ohio

Steel Vengeance combines the structure of a wooden coaster with modern steel track technology. The result is a ride packed with dramatic drops, inversions, and moments when riders feel lifted out of their seats.

Why we love it: The relentless pacing and variety of elements make every section of the ride memorable.

Best for: Experienced thrill seekers who love airtime and intense coaster layouts.

Best Wooden Coaster Pick

2. The Voyage — Holiday World, Indiana

The Voyage is a massive wooden roller coaster known for its long journey through wooded terrain. Its tunnels, sweeping turns, and sustained speed create an adventurous experience.

Why we love it: The ride feels like an expedition rather than a quick loop around a track.

Best for: Fans of traditional wooden coasters who enjoy speed and unpredictable movement.

Best High-Speed Adventure

3. Millennium Force — Cedar Point, Ohio

Millennium Force delivers a towering first drop and a smooth, fast journey along the Lake Erie shoreline. Its expansive layout makes the ride feel open and exhilarating.

Why we love it: The combination of height, speed, and scenery creates a unique experience.

Best for: Riders who prefer enormous hills and speed over multiple inversions.

Best Launch Experience

4. Maverick — Cedar Point, Ohio

Maverick proves that a coaster does not need to be the tallest in the park to deliver a thrilling experience. Its quick directional changes, launches, and low-to-the-ground elements create a fast-paced ride.

Why we love it: Every turn feels surprising, and the ride maintains its energy from start to finish.

Best for: Fans of rapid acceleration and technical coaster layouts.

Best Classic Thrill

5. The Beast — Kings Island, Ohio

The Beast is a legendary wooden coaster that carries riders through wooded sections of Kings Island. Its long layout and distinctive nighttime atmosphere have made it a favorite among coaster enthusiasts.

Why we love it: The secluded track and traditional wooden coaster character give it a personality unlike modern steel rides.

Best for: Riders who appreciate coaster history and adventurous terrain-based layouts.

How to Choose Your Next Coaster

If you love airtime, begin with Steel Vengeance. For classic wooden coaster excitement, consider The Voyage or The Beast. If speed is your priority, Millennium Force deserves a place near the top of your list.

Remember that ride availability, operating schedules, and safety requirements can change. Always consult official park information before traveling.
