Why Explore the Midwest?

The Midwest offers an impressive mix of record-setting thrill rides, historic wooden coasters, and family-friendly attractions. Ohio is famous for its coaster-heavy destinations, while Indiana, Illinois, Missouri, and Wisconsin offer their own unique amusement park experiences.

One of the biggest advantages of a Midwest coaster trip is the opportunity to combine multiple destinations into a single road trip. A carefully planned weekend can turn a regular vacation into a collection of unforgettable rides.

Meet the Fictional Team

Heartland Thrill Co. was imagined by three Midwest coaster enthusiasts: founder Alex Carter, travel planner Morgan Ellis, and ride reviewer Jamie Brooks. Together, they share one mission: making amusement park adventures easier to plan and more fun to experience.

Our editorial philosophy focuses on practical travel advice, accessible explanations, and the excitement of discovering a new favorite roller coaster.
